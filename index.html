Methodology

How I Approach Every Cut & Animation

Every project is handled with the same dedication—from client brand launches to passion animations.

01
🎯

Story & Retention First

Whether it’s a 15-second commercial ad or a YouTube wildlife doc, the first 3 seconds decide the rest. I eliminate dead air and maintain tension.

  • Strong visual hook design
  • Rhythm matching emotional intent
02
⚡

Animation & Motion Physics

Keyframes need life. Rooted in classical animation principles—squash, stretch, anticipation, and smooth bezier velocity curves.

  • Snappy velocity easing curves
  • Elasticity & natural momentum
03
🔊

Layered Sound Design

Video without sound design is only half alive. I layer impacts, ambient beds, foley textures, and risers to produce a tactile audio landscape.

  • Foley audio & environmental beds
  • Dynamic audio ducking for clarity
04
🎨

DaVinci Color Grading & Delivery

Color sets the mood. Using DaVinci Resolve, I balance exposure, match shots across scenes, and craft distinctive color palettes.

  • Accurate skin tones & tonal range
  • Targeted color management (Rec.709)
